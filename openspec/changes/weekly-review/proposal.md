## Why

A brain dump only helps if the user looks back at it. Today "what did I dump this week?" is refused by the search tool (a filed-date filter alone is not enough to list), nothing reminds the user to review their week, and an idea filed months ago stays open forever, with no way to put it away short of deleting it. `thought-status` left `archived` out on purpose for this feature (its decision 2), and `time-bundle` built its job runner and in-app notifications so this feature could reuse them.

## What Changes

- **"What did I dump this week?" in the chat.** A search with no query and only a filed-date filter (`since`, and a new `until` on the tool) now lists the thoughts filed in those days, instead of being refused. The list is grouped: open tasks first, then by category, newest filed first in each group, with a header per group. It takes up to 30 thoughts, with shorter summaries and its own size budget, sized to hold 30, and says how many more there are; a week with nothing filed says so. The search tool's description tells the model to use it for "what did I file / dump / add" questions and to answer in those groups, open tasks first. No new tool, no new store, no model call beyond the turn's own.
- **A weekly review conversation, off by default.** Settings gets a "Weekly review" section: a switch (off), a weekday and an hour (Sunday 18:00 by default), in the time zone of the browser that saves the setting (UTC until it is first saved). When it is on, a new job on `time-bundle`'s runner creates, at that time each week, a conversation titled "Weekly review, 1–8 Nov" that holds one message written by the server: the thoughts filed since the last weekly time (the 7 days before, the first time; at most the 21 days before, after missed weeks), grouped and cut at 30 as above. Creating it makes **no model call** and spends nothing on the user's key; the model runs only when the user replies, as any turn, under the spend cap. The conversation is made with no model: the user's first reply takes the default model they have at that moment, or asks them to pick one, as any conversation with no model does. A week with nothing filed makes no conversation. Changing the setting never brings a second review or resurfacing within 6 days of the last weekly time. One user's failing review does not hold up anyone else's.
  - The user is told with an in-app notification ("Weekly review"), which opens the conversation. It is not pushed to the browser.
  - The model reads the review as the app's note, marked as data, not as its own earlier answer or as instructions.
- **Resurfacing untouched ideas.** Once a week, at the same weekday and hour, up to 3 ideas the user has not touched for 30 days come back as in-app notifications, oldest first. An idea is untouched when it is an open thought of the category `idea`, and it was not filed, changed, added to, given a status, opened in the side sheet, kept, or resurfaced in those 30 days. Resurfacing is off by default, with its own switch beside the review's in the same Settings section.
  - Resurfacing only proposes. Each notification offers Keep, Archive, and Delete. Keep resets the idea's clock. Archive sets the status `archived`. Delete asks for confirmation and uses `thought-editing`'s `DELETE`. Nothing changes unless the user clicks: the job writes nothing to the thought, and reads its own notifications to know what it resurfaced. The review does not repeat the resurfaced ideas.
  - Opening a thought in the side sheet counts as viewing it (`thoughts.viewed_at`, a name kept apart from the chat's seen-thoughts note).
- **`archived`, a third status.** A thought can be `open`, `done`, or `archived`. The check constraint is widened by a migration. `PATCH /api/thoughts/{id}` takes `status: "archived"`. The sheet gets Archive (for an open thought) and Restore (for an archived one, back to open); Done is hidden while archived, and a done thought shows no Archive, so a finished task is never restored to open by mistake.
  - Every "open" list already asks for `open` by name (`thought-status` decision 2), so an archived thought drops out of the search's open filter, the Upcoming page, the calendar feed, and resurfacing with no change there. Reminders stop for an archived thought and start again when it is restored.
  - A search with no status filter still finds archived thoughts, marked `archived <date>`, as it finds done ones. The library offers "Archived" as a status filter and shows it on the row. The export carries the status as stored. A mark on an archived thought is refused, as on a done one, and a "Mark done" card proposed before the thought was archived is refused on confirm ("This thought is archived. Restore it first.") and keeps its button.
- New endpoints: `GET` and `PUT /api/settings/weekly-review` (which also say when the next weekly time is, shown in the section as "Next: Wed 18 Nov, 18:00"), `POST /api/thoughts/{id}/keep`. `GET /api/notifications` items gain `conversation_id` and `thought_status`. Errors use `{"error":{"code","message"}}`; another user's thought or conversation is 404 with the unknown-id body.
- Demo mode: the weekly review and resurfacing are off. The Settings section is not shown and its routes answer 404. Archive and Restore in the sheet still work.
- "Delete content" keeps the weekly review settings; "Delete account" deletes them. The "Delete content" confirmation says so.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `weekly-review`: the per-user setting, the weekly job, the review conversation and what its message holds, its notification, and demo mode.
- `idea-resurfacing`: when an idea is untouched, how often and how many come back, the notification with Keep, Archive, and Delete, and what each does.

### Modified Capabilities

- `chat-model-loadout`: modified requirement "A default model and a loadout": a new conversation starts with the default model, except a weekly review conversation, which the system makes with no model and which takes the default the user has at their first message there.
- `thought-store`: modified requirement "Every thought has an open or done status" (added by `thought-status`): `archived` is a status too, and the "Not a status" scenario uses another value. New requirement: a thought can be archived and restored, and an archived thought keeps everything else.
- `thought-search`: modified requirement "Filters and order" (as changed by `time-bundle`): a filed-date filter alone makes the query optional, such a list is grouped with open tasks first and has its own limit, and a search with no status filter returns every status.
- `conversation-agent`: modified requirements "The search tool filters by status and category", "The model knows which thoughts are done", and "A mark the system cannot apply goes back to the model" (all added by `thought-status`): the tool takes `until` and lists by filed days with the model steered to it, archived thoughts are marked like done ones, and a mark on an archived thought is refused. New requirement: the model reads a weekly review as the app's note, marked as data.
- `thought-filing`: modified requirement "A status change for a missing thought is refused" (added by `thought-status`): a confirm of a mark on a thought archived since the card was shown is refused and changes nothing.
- `conversations`: modified requirement "The sidebar lists conversations" (as changed by `quick-capture`): a weekly review conversation starts with the title "Weekly review, <days>" and is listed like any other.
- `chat-interface`: new requirement: a weekly review message shows in its conversation, and the user can reply to it.
- `thought-detail-view`: modified requirements "A thought opens in a side sheet" and "The sheet marks a thought done" (as changed by `thought-status`): the sheet shows an archived thought as archived, hides Done for it, and Archive and Restore join the ways the sheet changes a thought. New requirement: the sheet archives and restores.
- `thought-library` (added by `thought-library`): modified requirements "The list filters by category, tag, and status": an "Archived" scenario; and "The list follows changes made in the sheet": Archive and Restore reload the rows when saved, also after the sheet closed.
- `reminders` (added by `time-bundle`): modified requirement "Done and deleted thoughts send no reminders": an archived thought sends none either, and a restored one sends again.
- `notifications` (added by `time-bundle`): modified requirements "Notifications are listed in the app": a weekly review and a resurfaced idea are listed too, each with its own text, and a weekly review opens its conversation; and "A notification is pushed to the user's browsers": only reminder notifications are pushed.
- `account-data-deletion`: new requirement: the weekly review settings stay through "Delete content" and go with the account, and the confirmation says so.

## Impact

- Backend: `app/models/thought.py` (`THOUGHT_STATUSES` gains `archived`, the constraint, `viewed_at`), a new `app/models/review_settings.py`, `app/models/conversation.py` (the `review` message role in its docstring), time-bundle's notification model (`conversation_id`), two Alembic revisions (the status constraint and `viewed_at`; `review_settings` and `notifications.conversation_id`); `app/thoughts/store.py` (`check_status()` and `set_status()` take `archived`, `keep_thought()`, `mark_viewed()`), `app/thoughts/search.py` (the filed-days list and its order), `app/chat/tools.py` and `app/chat/prompt.py` (`until`, the grouped result, the tool description, the review note in `build_context()`), `app/chat/conversations.py` (`ends_answered()` for a review), `app/chat/compaction.py` (a review in the summary text), `app/chat/config.py` and `chat.yaml` (`list_limit`, `list_summary_chars`, `list_budget_chars`), a new `app/review/` (settings, schedule, grouping, the job), `app/jobs/__init__.py` (the `weekly-review` job), `app/routers/thoughts.py` (`ThoughtPatch.status` becomes a plain value checked by `check_status()`, so `archived` passes and every other value gets `status: must be open, done, or archived`; `mark_viewed()` on `GET`; `POST .../keep`), a new `app/routers/weekly_review.py`, `app/routers/notifications.py` (two fields, the current title of a resurfaced idea), `app/reminders.py` (not open sends nothing), `app/routers/conversations.py` (`confirm_proposal()` passes `refuse_archived`), and the `DELETE /api/me/content` and `DELETE /api/me` routes (the settings lock before the claim).
- Frontend: `lib/thoughts.ts` (`archived` in `THOUGHT_STATUSES` and `STATUS_LABELS`, `keepThought()`, which dispatches no `ff:thought-changed`), `components/chat/thought-sheet.tsx` (Archive for an open thought, Restore, Done hidden when archived), a new `components/weekly-review-settings.tsx` (with the next weekly time) and `lib/weekly-review.ts`, `app/settings/page.tsx`, `app/app/notifications/page.tsx` (the list reloads on `ff:thought-changed` and when the sheet closes, drops an answer from an older reload, and keeps the list when a reload fails) and a new `components/notifications/resurfaced-actions.tsx` (a 404 removes the item), `components/chat/chat.tsx` (the review message, and one sidebar refresh when an opened conversation is not in the sidebar's list) and `components/chat/message-list.tsx` (the review message), `components/delete-data-settings.tsx` (one phrase).
- API: `status: "archived"` on `PATCH` and in every thought answer; `until` on the search tool; `GET`/`PUT /api/settings/weekly-review` (with `next_review_at`); `POST /api/thoughts/{id}/keep`; `conversation_id` and `thought_status` on notification items; message role `review`; 409 `thought_archived` on a status confirm.
- Data: `thoughts.status` allows `archived`; `thoughts.viewed_at`; table `review_settings` (with `last_due_at`); `notifications.conversation_id`. No backfill.
- Jobs: the `weekly-review` job stops starting rows after a 10-second budget, so it never holds `time-bundle`'s one job loop long enough to make a reminder late.
- Cost: none on the user's key. The job, the review, resurfacing, and Keep make no model or embedding call. A reply in a review conversation is an ordinary turn.
- Depends on `thought-status` (the status column and the open-by-name lists), `time-bundle` (the runner, notifications, reminders), `thought-dates` (`since`/`until`, local days, `utc_now()`), `thought-editing` (`DELETE`, the delete dialog), `thought-library` (`THOUGHT_STATUSES`, `check_status()`, the status filter), `thought-export`, `stop-and-spend-cap` (the spend cap a reply runs under, the delete paths), `quick-capture` (an ordinary conversation is listed), `mcp-connectors` (the data marking), and `auth-modes` (demo mode). Archive after `thought-status`, `time-bundle`, `thought-library`, and `quick-capture` (design decision 15); the `chat-model-loadout` delta comes from the main spec and waits on nothing.
